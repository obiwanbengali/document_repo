Decision in brief

Do not equate graph clusters with clinical BN families. NHS Pathways transfers reveal structural communities but may blend unrelated presentations. Use clinically bounded proposed families, preserving evidence of algorithmic clusters, overlap and routing hotspots.

Clinical family allocations below are algorithm-assisted title/symptom-group screening, not clinically validated membership. The original Phase 2 Hybrid A allocations remain visible per pathway for traceability.

Examining workbook structure…

Aggregated totals can be duplicated across age, periods, channels or summaries. The page does not automatically sum ambiguous sheets or interpret pathway counts as unique patient journeys.

Safety and analytical limits

The proposed taxonomy is a screening classification based on pathway titles and symptom labels; it requires direct clinical verification. Some operational or unusual pathways cannot safely be classified using titles.

Cross-family transfer counts are graph relationships; they are neither posterior probabilities nor patient journey frequencies. The 111 Online workbook may represent a different time period/content version. Verify versions before comparing.

Family BN scope is not a one-to-one mapping of deterministic Pathways. The ontology, model variables and CPTs require separate clinical authoring and assurance.
