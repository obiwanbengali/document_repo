How the measures relate

E2E completion within group means the final observed pathway is in Revised ENT. It is then split into journeys that never leave Revised ENT and journeys that temporarily leave to an unsupported pathway before returning. Finish with handover out of scope is the complementary case where the final observed pathway is outside the family.
